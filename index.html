How this works

  • Derived, never typed. Results come from your inputs plus legal factors. Click any result's trace to see formula, inputs and source.
  • Law is data. Defaults, benchmarks and factors are built from the Official Journal texts and the Commission's workbooks, with the source row recorded.
  • Fail closed. If a case is ambiguous or unsupported, the tool refuses rather than guesses.
  • No data leaves your device. Calculations run in your browser; nothing you enter is sent anywhere.
